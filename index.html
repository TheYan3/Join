How it works

  1. 1

    You write an e-mail

    Describe your request in normal sentences — no form, no account, no special format. Mention a date if you need it by a certain day.

  2. 2

    The AI reads it

    It writes a short title, decides whether this is a technical task or a user story, sets a priority from the wording, and picks up any deadline you mentioned.

  3. 3

    A ticket appears

    Your request lands in the Triage column of the board, with you recorded as its author. You get a confirmation e-mail, and another one when the ticket moves.

Good to know

  • Ten requests per day

    To keep AI costs predictable, the collector creates 10 tickets per day in total. After that you can still send e-mails — our team then reviews them by hand instead of letting the AI create a ticket. The request page shows how many of today's ten are already used.

  • Your text goes to Google

    Messages are analysed with Google Gemini on its free tier. Google states that content sent through the free tier is used to improve its products. Please do not send anything confidential.

  • This is a demo

    Join is a student project from the Developer Akademie, not a production service. Tickets on the board are publicly visible. Use made-up data when you try it out.

Try it yourself

Write to join-issues@gmx.de in your own words. Here is a sample mail and everything that gets pulled out of it.

To join-issues@gmx.de

Subject Login stopped working

Hi, since this morning none of our staff can sign in. After entering the password nothing happens at all. This is blocking our whole production, we urgently need a fix by 05/09/2026.

What ends up on the ticket

Is it a real request?
Yes — spam, adverts and out-of-office replies are recognised and never become tickets
Title
“Staff login fails after password entry”
Category
Technical Task — a bug report, as opposed to a User Story for feature wishes
Priority
Urgent — taken from wording like “urgently” and “blocking our whole production”
Due date
05/09/2026 — picked up from the text; left empty when no date is mentioned
Summary
Two or three plain sentences, greetings stripped out
Author
Your e-mail address, marked as an external request

The ticket then appears in the Triage column with a note that it was generated by AI.
