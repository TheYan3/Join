Write to us in plain language. An AI reads your message, works out what kind of request it is, how urgent it is and when it is due — then files it on our kanban board. You get a confirmation as soon as your ticket exists.

How it works

  1. 1

    You write an e-mail

    Describe your request in normal sentences — no form, no account, no special format. Mention a date if you need it by a certain day.

  2. 2

    The AI reads it

    It writes a short title, decides whether this is a technical task or a user story, sets a priority from the wording, and picks up any deadline you mentioned.

  3. 3

    A ticket appears

    Your request lands in the Triage column of the board, with you recorded as its author. You get a confirmation e-mail, and another one when the ticket moves.

Good to know

  • Ten requests per day

    To keep AI costs predictable, the collector accepts 10 requests per day in total. If the limit is reached, you still get a reply telling you so — your message is not silently dropped.

  • Your text goes to Google

    Messages are analysed with Google Gemini on its free tier. Google states that content sent through the free tier is used to improve its products. Please do not send anything confidential.

  • This is a demo

    Join is a student project from the Developer Akademie, not a production service. Tickets on the board are publicly visible. Use made-up data when you try it out.

Try it yourself

Send a short mail to join-issues@gmx.de — for example: “The contact list should be sortable by company. Not urgent.” Then open the board and watch it show up in Triage.
